# This 2,300-Year-Old Machine Makes Water Climb UPHILL 🌀 How many pockets?

**Durata:** 32.3s

## Descriere YouTube

The Archimedes screw: tilt a spiral to ~30°, turn it, and every pocket of water is trapped and forced uphill — no pump, no pressure, just geometry. Today cities run it backwards as a hydro turbine.

3D reconstruction by Ancient Tech. How many water pockets did you count? Comment A or B.

Sources: Vitruvius X.6; Rorres, 'The Turn of the Screw' (2000).
