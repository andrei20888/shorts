# How Egypt Stood Up a 455-TON Obelisk… With Sand 🏛️ #AncientTech

**Durata:** 51.4s

## Descriere YouTube

No crane, no engine: drag the obelisk to a sand-filled pit, let the sand out slowly, and gravity tilts 455 tons of granite onto its pedestal. The biggest one ever attempted (1,090 t) cracked and still lies in the Aswan quarry.
