# Egypt Stood Up a 455-TON Obelisk With SAND 🏛️ Should we finish the broken one?

**Durata:** 35.5s

## Descriere YouTube

No crane, no engine: drag the obelisk to a sand-filled pit, let the sand out slowly, and gravity tilts 455 tons of granite onto its pedestal — with one chance to get it right. The biggest one ever attempted (1,090 t) cracked and still lies in the Aswan quarry.
