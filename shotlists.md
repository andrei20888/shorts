# Abs For Real: shot lists for one shooting day (clips 1–10)

Everything below is written so I can cut it without asking you anything: each line is its own take,
with a verbal slate, so Whisper + the edit script can find the best take automatically.

## Before you press record (applies to every clip)

**Camera**
- Phone vertical, 9:16, 4K or 1080p, **30 fps constant** (turn off "auto frame rate" / HDR / cinematic mode).
- **Lock exposure and white balance** (tap and hold on your face, then lock). Keep the same lock all day so the clips match in colour without heavy grading.
- Phone at eye level, 1.0–1.3 m away. Face in the upper-middle: eyes at ~40% of the height. Leave room above the head for the hook text and keep your chin above the middle of the frame, so subtitles at 62% never touch your mouth.
- Light from the front (window or a lamp behind the phone). No window behind you.
- Same spot and framing for all talking-head takes. Change the top (3 outfits for the day) so clips don't look identical: outfit 1 = clips 1, 2, 5; outfit 2 = clips 3, 4, 7, 8; outfit 3 = clips 6, 9, 10.

**Sound**
- Quiet room, no fan, no fridge hum. Lav mic if you have one; otherwise stay within 1.3 m of the phone.
- Record 10 seconds of room silence at the start of the day (file `raw/00_roomtone.mov`): it feeds the noise reduction.

**How to deliver each take (this is what makes the edit fast)**
- Say the slate, then 1 second of silence, then the line, then 1 second of silence, then stop recording.
  Slate = clip number, line number, take: "one, three, take two".
- 3 takes per line. Take 1 neutral, take 2 more energy, take 3 faster and shorter (cut the "ands").
- Look at the lens, not the screen. Speak like you're explaining it to one friend.
- At the end of each clip, also record the whole script in one go, twice (slate "one, full, take one"): it gives me natural flow between lines.
- Don't restart a take mid-line. If you stumble, keep rolling, pause 1 second, say the line again.

**File names** (so the folder is self-describing)
- Talking head: `raw/01/01_hookA_t1.mov`, `raw/01/01_L03_t2.mov`, `raw/01/01_full_t1.mov`
- B-roll: `broll/01/01_b1_chat.mov`, `broll/01/01_b2_scroll.mov` … (number + short name from the list)
- Screen recordings: enable "show touches" if your phone has it, hide notifications (Do Not Disturb), battery full, no personal names in chats.

**B-roll rules**
- Every B-roll shot: 10–15 s long, static or one slow handheld move, no fast pans.
- Screen recordings in portrait, same phone, with the Abs For Real screens in their real state. If a screen isn't built yet, record a Figma/prototype screen or skip it; I'll leave the face on.
- Training shots: side angle at hip height, plus one 45° front angle. Mat on the floor, plain background.
