## Fact notes
- Recent flagship phone chips: ~16–20 billion transistors (A16: 16B, A17 Pro: 19B) → "over fifteen billion".
- Human hair ≈ 50–100 µm, red blood cell ≈ 7–8 µm, typical virus ≈ 20–300 nm (SARS‑CoV‑2 ≈ 100 nm).
- Modern transistor features: fins ≈ 5–7 nm wide, gate pitch ≈ 45–50 nm → "even smaller than a virus".
